ACatechism of Perseverance

with Father Emily

"We are forced to agree with those who hold that the chief cause of the present indifference and, as it were, infirmity of soul, and the serious evils that result from it, is to be found above all in ignorance of things divine… St. Paul places the foundation of holiness and sound morals upon a knowledge of divine things which holds in check evil desires." St. Pius X · Encyclical Acerbo Nimis

✟

This class will benefit all the members of the family to refresh their memory and help them to better know their Faith. A better knowledge of the Mysteries of our Faith will increase our Love for God and our zeal to work our sanctification and the sanctification of our families.

As St. Ignatius of Loyola says:

"Here it will be to ask for an intimate knowledge of Our Lord, who has become man for me, that I may love Him more and follow Him more closely."

May our dear parents encourage their children, especially the teenagers, who have received the sacrament of Confirmation, to attend this class of Catechism of Perseverance for the good of their souls.

Sincerely yours in Jesus, Mary, and Joseph,

Fr. Jacques Emily Pastor
